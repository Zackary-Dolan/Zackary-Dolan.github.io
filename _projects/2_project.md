The task was to create a the base for a game called survivor.io. We created the game following the instructions of our lecturer.
Once that was complete we were then tasked to create a series of new items and pickups that would affect gameplay in interesting ways.
I opted for a minigun that has an insanely high rate of fire but a short time for the powerup.

 The idea is simple but my lack of knowledge at the time led to several learning moments that I carry with me even now as I continue to learn.
